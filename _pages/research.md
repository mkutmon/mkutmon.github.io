<div class="r-lead">
  My research is in <strong>network biology</strong>. I develop network-based approaches to integrate diverse types of data – multi-omics, clinical and imaging data – across biological scales, from molecular interactions to physiological systems and the whole body. Pathways, networks and disease maps provide the structure to connect these data and to interpret them in the context of disease mechanisms.
</div>
