Hello!
I am an Associate Professor at the **Maastricht Centre for Systems Biology and Bioinformatics ([MaCSBio]([https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/research/macsbio](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/research/maastricht-centre-systems-biology-and-bioinformatics)))** at Maastricht University.

My research is in **network biology**: I develop computational approaches that integrate diverse types of data – from multi-omics to clinical and imaging data – across biological scales, from molecular interactions to physiological systems and the whole body. Biological pathways, networks and disease maps form the backbone of this work, providing a structured way to connect heterogeneous data and to interpret it in the context of disease mechanisms. I apply these approaches to complex, multi-system conditions such as post-COVID, inflammatory skin diseases and pancreatic cancer.

I am a strong advocate of open science. I have long been involved in the [WikiPathways](https://www.wikipathways.org) and [Disease Maps](https://disease-maps.io) communities and lead the development of open-source tools such as [PathVisio](https://pathvisio.org) and different Cytoscape apps, so that knowledge about disease mechanisms is accessible and reusable by the wider scientific community.

Half of my position is dedicated to education. I am involved in the Educational Programme Committee and Board of Admissions of the **MSc Systems Biology and Bioinformatics** programme and teach systems and network biology courses across bachelor and master programmes at Maastricht University (see [Teaching](/teaching/)).

I am also active in the wider community, for example within [ISCB](https://www.iscb.org), as co-organiser of the Network Biology (NetBio) COSI at ISMB, and as [Women in Data Science (WiDS)](https://www.widsworldwide.org) Maastricht ambassador.
